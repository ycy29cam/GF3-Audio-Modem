CUED Division of Chaotic Modems

Pioneering audio transmission... one poorly-synchronized tone at a time.

About Us

Born in a lab where the air smells faintly of burned solder and late-night despair, the Division of Chaotic Modems is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what should reasonably be transmitted over audio using OFDM.

Our Mission

To develop an interoperable, error-prone, acoustically aggressive file transfer system that makes even dial-up modems look advanced. We embrace echoes, packet loss, and awkward pauses between symbols.

Research Highlights

  • Invented OFDM-based data encoding using only the "clap" sound from a lecture theatre.
  • Achieved a groundbreaking 12% success rate in transmitting corrupted .txt files across a desk.
  • Developed the world's first modem that requires absolute silence, motionlessness, and spiritual focus.
